This is an independent React implementation inspired by [bilacuti.my](https://www.bilacuti.my/), originally created by [Gaddafi Rusli](https://x.com/gaddafirusli). It is not the original Bilacuti source repository and does not imply endorsement or ownership of that service. Original guide links and attribution are retained in the calendar components.

The bundled calendar data is a snapshot of Malaysian public and school holiday dates for 2026 and 2027 observed on bilacuti.my during development. It is not fetched or updated automatically. Check official Malaysian government and education announcements before relying on dates for planning.
